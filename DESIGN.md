# Into the Pitch: design direction

> Dial: ENERGY 3 / RHYTHM 3 / MOTION 2
>
> Reading this as: a broadcast tactical cam for learning attacks. The pitch is the entire product. Chrome is HUD overlaid on the field, not an app shell around it.

## Reinvention (why the old UI failed)

The previous UI was a dashboard: match bar + selectors + pitch card + clipboard sidebar. That reads as generic SaaS with a green rectangle. Replacing paint without changing composition still looked like slop.

This direction kills the sidebar, kills the card well, and kills the chalk-gold-on-everything accent. Composition is new.

## Hero Extraction

- Mode: floodlit night default (training daylight is the alternate)
- Focal: full-viewport top-down pitch, edge to edge
- Type: Panchang (Fontshare) for numbers and brand; Satoshi (Fontshare) for coaching copy. All sans.
- Text sits ON the pitch as HUD (lower-third + left rail), not in a separate column
- Gravity: pitch fills the frame; HUD hugs edges
- Palette: grass + kit colors on the field only; chrome is ink/bone/white
- Nav: left vertical rail (formation + plays)
- Motif: oversized step numeral in the lower-third

## Layout

1. Pitch canvas: fixed, full viewport, zero page chrome around it
2. Corner bug: logomark + wordmark (tiny)
3. Theme: ghost control, top-right
4. Left rail: formation select + vertical play list
5. Lower-third: giant step number, caption, input legend, filmstrip of steps, transport
6. No sidebar. No clipboard card. No floating glass dock on the grass.

## Typography

- **Panchang** (Fontshare): wordmark, formation numerals, step numbers, HUD labels. Geometric display with sports-poster weight.
- **Satoshi** (Fontshare): captions, play titles in the rail, coaching body. Clean sans for a tactical HUD, not an editorial page.
- No serifs. This is a broadcast desk, not a magazine.


## Motion

- Lower-third caption crossfade on step change
- Transport hover press
- No page-load blur stagger theatre
- Pitch animation remains the main motion
